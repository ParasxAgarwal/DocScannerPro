Doc Scanner Pro is a native Android document utility for capturing, organizing, editing and exporting paper documents without an account or cloud service.

Capture documents with automatic edge detection, perspective correction and multi-page scanning. Use dedicated workflows for documents, books, receipts, identity cards, business cards and barcodes.

Create and work with local documents:
• Scan pages and combine them into multi-page documents
• Correct perspective, crop, rotate and enhance captures
• Digitize books with two-page capture and spread splitting
• Extract text with on-device OCR
• Create searchable PDFs
• Merge, split, reorder and extract PDF pages
• Export to PDF, DOCX, Markdown, text, CSV and JSON where supported
• Annotate pages, add signatures and redact sensitive content
• Search document titles, OCR text, notes and tags
• Organize documents with folders, favorites and local metadata
• Keep private documents in the local vault
• Import photos and documents with Android file and photo pickers

Privacy is part of the architecture. There is no sign-in, cloud sync, backend, advertising SDK or telemetry service in the application. Documents stay on the device unless you deliberately export or share them through Android.

Doc Scanner Pro is built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, OpenCV and other open-source components. See the repository for the complete dependency and license information.
